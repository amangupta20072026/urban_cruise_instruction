## 1. YOUR RIGHT TO DELETE YOUR ACCOUNT

If you have created an account on the Urban Cruise Mobile Application or Website (whether as a Customer, Driver or Vendor Partner), you have the right to request that:

1. Your account be permanently deactivated and closed; and
2. The personal data associated with your account be deleted or irreversibly anonymised, save for such information as we are required to retain under applicable law (as further described in Clause 5 below).

You may exercise this right at any time, free of charge. Deletion is permanent and cannot be reversed.

## 2. HOW TO REQUEST DELETION

You may submit a deletion request through any of the following channels. All three channels result in the same processing timeline and the same scope of deletion.

### Option A — From within the Urban Cruise Mobile Application

1. Open the Urban Cruise Mobile Application and sign in if prompted.
2. Navigate to **Profile → Settings → Delete Account**.
3. Read the summary of data to be deleted and data to be retained.
4. Tap **Delete Account**. You will be redirected to this deletion request page to complete the request.

## 3. IDENTITY VERIFICATION

To protect you against unauthorised deletion of your account, we will verify your identity before processing your request. Verification shall be conducted through one or more of the following methods:

1. A One-Time Password (OTP) sent to your registered mobile number;
2. A confirmation email sent to your registered email address;
3. Additional documentation, in cases where the OTP or email verification cannot be completed (for example, if the registered mobile number is no longer active).

If we are unable to verify your identity through reasonable means, we may decline the request in order to protect the security of the account.

---

## 4. STEPS TO COMPLETE BEFORE DELETION

Before your account can be closed, please note that certain matters must be resolved. Where any of the following apply to your account, our support team will contact you to complete them and, if necessary, will hold your deletion request until they are resolved:

1. **Active or upcoming bookings.** Any confirmed booking must be either completed or cancelled (subject to the cancellation charges disclosed in the applicable booking confirmation and in the Terms & Conditions).
2. **Outstanding dues or refunds.** Any unpaid invoice, security deposit, damage recovery or pending refund must be settled or credited to you.
3. **Wallet balance, referral credits, loyalty points.** Non-transferable balances shall be forfeited upon deletion. Where required by law, transferable balances shall be paid out to your registered bank account.
4. **Drivers and Vendor Partners.** Any active trip, unsettled payout, KYC-related dispute, insurance claim or safety investigation must be resolved before the account is closed.
5. **Corporate accounts.** Where the account was created under a corporate travel programme, we may require confirmation from the authorised administrator of that corporate account before processing the deletion.

The Mobile Application and the deletion form will guide you through the resolution of the above where possible.

## 5. WHAT IS DELETED, AND WHAT IS RETAINED

### 5.1 Data deleted or irreversibly anonymised

Within thirty (30) days from the date on which your identity is verified and the pre-deletion steps in Clause 4 are completed, the following data shall be deleted or irreversibly anonymised:

- Your name, profile photograph, email address and secondary contact numbers
- Saved home, office and favourite addresses
- Push notification and messaging tokens (including Firebase Cloud Messaging tokens)
- Marketing preferences and consent records
- App-usage analytics and crash logs that are linked to your identity
- Feedback, ratings and reviews to the extent linked to your identity (the content itself may be retained in an anonymised, aggregated form)
- Referral connections and social identifiers
- **Drivers and Vendor Partners:** profile information, availability preferences, vehicle allocations and non-statutory KYC copies not required by law

### 5.2 Data retained for legal, tax, safety and regulatory purposes

Certain records must be retained for the periods and reasons set out below, in accordance with applicable Indian law. Such retained data shall be stored securely, access-restricted, and used only for the stated purposes.

| Category | Retention period | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| Booking, trip and invoice records | 8 (eight) years from the end of the relevant financial year | Income Tax Act, 1961; Central Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017 |
| Payment and transaction references (masked) | 8 (eight) years | Reserve Bank of India directions; tax audit |
| KYC records of Drivers and Vendor Partners | 8 (eight) years from the end of the engagement | Motor Vehicles Act, 1988; Income Tax Act, 1961; regulatory verification |
| Fraud, safety incident and dispute records | Until the matter is closed and applicable statutory limitation periods have expired | Fraud prevention; safety; legal defence |
| Chat, call and support logs relating to safety incidents | As required by the investigating authority or by law | Safety; law enforcement |
| Records subject to a legal hold | Until the legal hold is lifted | Court, tribunal or regulator directions |
| Aggregated / anonymised analytics | Indefinite | Product improvement (not linkable to you) |

Upon expiry of the applicable retention period, the retained data shall also be deleted or irreversibly anonymised.

## 6. THIRD PARTIES

Where your data has been shared with a third-party service provider engaged by us to process such data on our behalf (as described in the Privacy Policy), we shall delete such data from our own systems and instruct the concerned service provider to delete the data held by them, save for data they are required to retain under law or under their own regulatory obligations.

Deletion of your Urban Cruise account does not delete your account or data held by any third-party login provider (e.g., Google), payment provider (e.g., UPI PSP, card network, bank), or telecom provider through whom you communicated with us. You must contact such third parties directly if you wish to delete data held by them.

## 8. IMPORTANT NOTICES

1. **Permanence.** Deletion is permanent. Once your account is deleted, your booking history, ratings, referral credits, loyalty benefits and Driver / Vendor Partner performance records will no longer be accessible to you.
2. **Re-registration.** You may create a new account with the same mobile number or email in future, but no data from the deleted account shall be restored.
3. **Deactivation is not deletion.** Temporary deactivation, disabling or suspension of an account is not the same as deletion under this page. If you wish to only temporarily deactivate your account, please use the corresponding option in the Mobile Application or write to us at support@urbancruise.in.
4. **No fee.** We do not charge any fee for processing a deletion request.
5. **No prejudice to law.** Nothing on this page limits your rights, or our obligations, under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 or any other applicable law.
