### D. INFORMATION COLLECTED VIA MOBILE APPLICATION PERMISSIONS

The Mobile Application requests Device permissions only for functions that support the Services. Each permission is preceded by an in-app prominent disclosure prior to the operating system prompt. Permissions requested may include:

- **Location (foreground and, for Drivers on active trips, background)** — for pick-up detection, live trip tracking, driver dispatch, ETA and safety. A persistent notification is shown when background location is in use. Background collection stops when the trip ends or the Driver goes off-duty.
- **Camera and Photos** — for profile photograph, KYC document capture, and issue / damage evidence upload.
- **Notifications** — for booking, dispatch, driver-arrival and safety alerts.
- **Contacts** — only when you choose to add a co-passenger or emergency contact via the Android Contact Picker; the Mobile Application does not request broad access to your address book.
- **Storage / Media** — via the system photo picker only, for the uploads listed above.

We do not request SMS-reading, call-log, or microphone permissions. If any additional permission becomes necessary in future, this Policy and the in-app disclosures will be updated accordingly.

## 3. LOCATION DATA — ADDITIONAL DISCLOSURE

- **Customers** — location is collected only when the Mobile Application is in use (foreground), for pick-up detection and ETA display. You may enter locations manually and revoke the permission at any time from your Device settings, though doing so may limit certain features.
- **Drivers** — location is collected in the foreground and, where applicable, in the background while a trip is active or the Driver is on-duty, for the purposes of dispatch, live tracking (which is shared with the Customer and Urban Cruise operations for safety), route optimisation, ETA, and after-trip verification of distance/time. Background collection is disabled when the Driver goes off-duty or ends the trip. A foreground service notification is displayed while background collection is active, in compliance with Android platform requirements.
- Location data is retained as per Clause 8 and used only for the purposes stated in this Policy.

## 8. RETENTION OF INFORMATION

We retain your information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, including for the purposes of satisfying any legal, regulatory, tax, accounting, audit, fraud-prevention, safety-investigation or reporting requirements. Indicative retention periods:

| Category | Retention period |
|---|---|
| Active account profile data | While the account is active |
| Booking, trip, invoice and tax records | 8 (eight) years from the end of the relevant financial year (Income Tax Act, 1961 and GST law) |
| KYC records of Drivers and Vendor Partners | 8 (eight) years after the end of the engagement |
| Communication logs, chat transcripts, call recordings, support tickets | Up to 3 (three) years |
| Location traces (per trip) | 12 (twelve) months, then aggregated or anonymised |
| Analytics and crash logs | As per the retention defaults of the analytics provider (typically 14 months) |
| Marketing consent records | Until withdrawn + 2 (two) years |
| Records relating to fraud or safety incidents, or subject to legal hold | Until the matter is closed and applicable statutory limitation periods have expired |

Upon the expiry of the applicable retention period, information is deleted or irreversibly anonymised.

## 12. SECURITY

We adopt reasonable security practices and procedures in accordance with the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011 and industry standards, including:

- Encryption in transit (HTTPS / TLS) for all traffic between the Platform and our servers
- Encryption at rest for sensitive data fields
- OTP-based authentication and secure token storage on Devices (using platform Keychain / Keystore)
- Role-based access controls (RBAC), least-privilege access and access-audit logging for internal systems
- Screen-capture protection on sensitive screens
- Regular security assessments of the Platform and of TPSPs
- A defined incident-response process

Despite our safeguards, no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure. In the event of a personal-data breach, we will notify the Data Protection Board of India and affected users in the manner and within the timelines required by law.
